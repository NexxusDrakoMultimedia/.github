# Acknowledgements

Nexxus Drako Multimedia was created on the stolen lands of the Jagera and Turrbal peoples, in what is now called Brisbane. Sovereignty was never ceded. This always was, and always will be, Aboriginal land. We pay our respects to Jagera and Turrbal Elders past and present, and to all Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples.

We acknowledge that colonisation is ongoing, and we support First Nations-led movements for land back, treaty, truth-telling, and justice.

## Community and collaborators

This work is shaped by many communities, creators, researchers, and supporters.

We acknowledge the broader creative, technical, and fandom communities that have helped inspire, challenge, and strengthen the work. This includes the wider game-dev, modding, art, music, and community-building communities that make experimentation and independent creative practice possible.

We also recognise the importance of preserving digital history, community memory, and older creative work. The archive and preservation work within this umbrella is part of a wider effort to maintain access to projects, tools, and cultural artifacts that deserve continued visibility.

## Creative values

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is queer-run and queer-made. The work here is shaped by trans, nonbinary, intersex, and queer lived experience. We centre inclusion, safety, and respect rather than tolerance.

We do not tolerate transphobia, homophobia, biphobia, acephobia, intersexphobia, racism, or other forms of harmful discrimination. These values are part of the creative culture of the studio and the community around it.

## Thanks

Thanks to the communities, collaborators, and supporters who continue to help build this work, whether through direct collaboration, feedback, encouragement, or simply by taking part in the creative culture around it.

This project exists because of community, craft, and care.
