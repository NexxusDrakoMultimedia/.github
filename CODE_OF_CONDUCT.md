# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is a queer-led, trans-inclusive, and artist-first creative environment, and the work here is shaped by that ethos. This project and its community are governed by the values of respect, dignity, and creative safety.

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, sexual identity and orientation, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or mental health.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community. Harassment, abuse, intimidation, exclusion, or demeaning behaviour are not tolerated.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contribute to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- being respectful and constructive in communication
- treating people with dignity regardless of identity, background, or experience level
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- being careful with language, especially when discussing identities, disability, gender, sexuality, race, or mental health
- using people's correct names and pronouns
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from the experience
- focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall community
- helping maintain a safe, collaborative, and creative environment

Examples of unacceptable behaviour include:

- transphobia, misogyny, homophobia, biphobia, acephobia, intersexphobia, racism, or any other bigotry
- harassment, intimidation, or targeted abuse, whether public or private
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- deliberate misgendering or deadnaming, and outing or threatening to out someone
- the use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- discriminatory language or repeated disruptive behaviour
- hateful, degrading, or exclusionary conduct
- publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- behaviour that makes a community member feel unsafe or unwelcome
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities

Community leaders and maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behaviour, and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate. In serious cases, participation may be restricted or removed without warning.

The standards here are enforced consistently. This project does not permit debate or negotiation around bigotry, harassment, or harmful discrimination.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces. Examples of representing our community include using an official email address, posting via an official social media account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainers through the available project contact channels. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident. Reporters will not face retaliation for good-faith reports.

If a report involves a serious safety concern, please prioritise immediate practical safety and seek local support or emergency assistance if needed.

## Enforcement guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining the consequences for any action they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct. Behaviour that is clearly bigoted or targeted harassment may skip straight to a later step.

### 1. Correction

**Community impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other behaviour deemed unprofessional or unwelcome in the community.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

**Community impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, for a specified period of time. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces as well as external channels like social media. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

**Community impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period of time. No public or private interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the Code of Conduct, is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

**Community impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

## Support and safety

If you are in crisis or need urgent support, contact your local emergency services or a local crisis helpline. If you need a general support or crisis-directory resource, use a trusted local service or mental-health support line.

## Statement of inclusion

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is queer-run and queer-made. Trans, nonbinary, intersex, asexual, aromantic, bi, pan, lesbian, gay, and every other queer identity is welcome here, and actively supported.

Trans rights are human rights.

This is not a place for tolerance of hate. This is a place for safety, respect, and creative belonging.
