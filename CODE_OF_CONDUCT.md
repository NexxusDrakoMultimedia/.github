# Code of Conduct

This project and its community are governed by the values of respect, dignity, and creative safety. Nexxus Drako Multimedia is a queer-led, trans-inclusive, and artist-first creative environment, and the work here is shaped by that ethos.

We are committed to building a community that is welcoming, safe, and professionally respectful. Harassment, abuse, intimidation, exclusion, or demeaning behaviour are not tolerated.

## Our standards

We expect everyone in the community to:

- be respectful and constructive in communication
- treat people with dignity regardless of identity, background, or experience level
- avoid harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- be careful with language, especially when discussing identities, disability, gender, sexuality, race, or mental health
- help maintain a safe, collaborative, and creative environment

We do not accept:

- transphobia, misogyny, homophobia, biphobia, acephobia, or any other bigotry
- harassment, intimidation, or targeted abuse
- discriminatory language or repeated disruptive behaviour
- hateful, degrading, or exclusionary conduct
- behaviour that makes a community member feel unsafe or unwelcome

## Enforcement

The maintainers reserve the right to remove, restrict, or take action against any behaviour that breaks this code of conduct. In serious cases, participation may be restricted or removed without warning.

The standards here are enforced consistently, and the project does not permit debate or negotiation around bigotry, harassment, or harmful discrimination.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, please report it to the project maintainers through the available project contact channels.

If a report involves a serious safety concern, please prioritise immediate practical safety and seek local support or emergency assistance if needed.

## Support and safety

If you are in crisis or need urgent support, contact your local emergency services or a local crisis helpline. If you need a general support or crisis-directory resource, use a trusted local service or mental-health support line.

## Statement of inclusion

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is queer-run and queer-made. Trans, nonbinary, intersex, asexual, aromantic, bi, pan, lesbian, gay, and every other queer identity is welcome here, and actively supported.

Trans rights are human rights.

This is not a place for tolerance of hate. This is a place for safety, respect, and creative belonging.
