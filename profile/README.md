# Nexxus Drako Multimedia

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is the creative umbrella for Pyra Drake, a Brisbane-based autistic trans woman, game designer, musician, storyteller, and digital creator. This is where the weird little sparks become worlds, games, music, and stories.

It is part brand, part archive, part game studio, part creative playground, and part community space. The work is built around imagination, craft, experimentation, and a deep love of making things that feel alive.

## The creative heart

My work keeps circling back to a few core themes:

- storytelling with emotion and worldbuilding at the centre
- community-driven creative practice
- learning across many disciplines and turning that process into art
- building games, systems, and identities that feel lived in
- turning odd little ideas into something public, collaborative, and alive

The wider universe of my work is centred on the Itavran Union, a fictional space-faring civilisation with its own species, politics, and mythic history.

The design-system ties all of that together visually, giving the whole creative world a clear identity: rich, theatrical, tactile, and unmistakably mine.

## Personal note

The Nexxus Drako name has a long history online, growing from early web communities, indie projects, and creative experimentation into a broader practice spanning games, music, design, and storytelling.

Across different eras, the work has included cricket games, open scoreboards, modding projects, nation-building experiments, and ongoing efforts to turn imagination into something collaborative and alive.

In recent years, the focus has shifted toward professional game development, music production, creative systems, and worldbuilding centred on the Itavran Union.
