# Nexxus Drako Multimedia

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is the creative studio and umbrella brand of Pyra Drake — a Brisbane-based founder, creative director, game developer, musician, storyteller, designer, and digital media practitioner.

Operating under the names Pyra Drake, KitsuNet, and formerly Jarokn, the brand brings together independent development, community storytelling, music, design, worldbuilding, and digital preservation into a single artistic identity.

This is more than a single project or platform. It is a creative practice built around making meaningful, expressive work across games, music, culture, and interactive storytelling.

## Our position

Nexxus Drako Multimedia exists to build worlds, games, and experiences that are expressive, human, and distinctly personal.

We work at the intersection of:

- game design and development
- worldbuilding and narrative systems
- music, sound, and atmosphere
- content creation and community engagement
- design, branding, and digital identity
- preservation of creative history and legacy work

Our studio ethos is rooted in creative independence, experimentation, and long-form worldbuilding. We build work that is not just functional, but alive with story, systems, and personality.

## Studio identity

Nexxus Drako Multimedia is a queer-led creative practice shaped by independence, experimentation, and ambition. It is built around the idea that creative work can be both deeply personal and universally resonant.

The studio is grounded in a founder-led creative direction, with Pyra Drake acting as the driving force behind the umbrella's artistic and strategic identity. That vision brings together technical craft, narrative imagination, and public-facing community work into one coherent creative ecosystem.

## History and direction

The Nexxus Drako name has grown from a long-running internet identity and indie development past into a broader creative studio practice. Earlier work spans cricket games, writing, community tools, simulation ideas, and experimental project concepts, many of which helped define the creative foundations behind the modern umbrella.

Over time, the focus has shifted toward a more intentional creative studio model: original games, original music, worldbuilding, design systems, public-facing creative work, and long-form project building. The brand now operates as a creative environment for ideas to mature across disciplines instead of being confined to a single genre or platform.

This direction reflects a broader philosophy: build things with lasting personality, maintain creative ownership, and create worlds that can evolve across games, music, design, and community.
